# GlowBal Brand Entity & Authority Checklist

## 1. Goal
Establish clear, unambiguous entity recognition for **GlowBal** across Google Knowledge Graph, Search Engine entities, and AI search systems. Distinguish GlowBal as a leading EdTech and scholarship guidance platform.

---

## 2. On-Page Entity Signals (Verified & Normalized)

- [x] **Consistent Brand Name:** Unified as `GlowBal` (or `GlowBal Education`) across `<title>`, OpenGraph `site_name`, Organization schema, and footer copyright.
- [x] **Canonical Domain:** Hard-redirect all traffic to `https://glowbal-education.com/`.
- [x] **Structured Data (Schema.org):**
  - **Organization:** Name, alternateName, URL, logo, description.
  - **WebSite:** Name, URL, inLanguage (`['vi', 'en']`), publisher entity reference.
  - **CollegeOrUniversity:** Canonical URL, verified name, location, and official university site.
  - **Person:** Advisor name, university affiliation, subject expertise.
  - **Article:** Headline, author/publisher entity, truthful `datePublished` and `dateModified`.
- [x] **Trust Pages & Company Transparency:**
  - `/about`: Company mission, founder/team members, product vision.
  - `/how-it-works`: Transparent 3-stage, 14-step guidance model.
  - `/privacy` & `/terms`: Legal terms and privacy policies.
