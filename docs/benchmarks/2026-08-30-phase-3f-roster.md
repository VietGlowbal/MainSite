## Required reference fields

For each applicable programme, the independent review record must cover:

- programme identity and credential;
- programme status;
- tuition/fees for the target cycle;
- application deadline and intake;
- English-language requirement;
- major admissions requirements;
- funding/scholarship data where applicable.

Each value/state must retain its source URL or document identifier, evidence
locator, audience, academic cycle, reviewer, and review timestamp. A missing
field must be reviewed as a semantic state, not silently scored as absent.
