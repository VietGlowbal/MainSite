# UK Data Science Master's Scholarships for Vietnamese Students (2026/27)
## Short answer
Three funding routes matter most for a Vietnamese student doing a UK Data Science master's: **Chevening** (fully funded, but competitive and work-experience based), **GREAT Scholarships** (a £10,000 partial award at selected universities), and **university merit scholarships** (partial tuition discounts you apply for alongside your course). Note one thing up front: because Vietnam is **not** a Commonwealth member, Commonwealth Scholarships do not apply.

## Who this guide is for
This is for Vietnamese graduates planning a one-year taught master's in Data Science (or AI/analytics) in the UK, who want to know which scholarships are realistic, what they are worth, and how to qualify.

## What to know first
- **[Chevening](https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/vietnam/) — fully funded.** Covers tuition for a one-year master's, a monthly living stipend, return flights, and visa costs. Around **20 awards** are offered to Vietnam each cycle. You need an undergraduate degree equivalent to a UK 2:1 and roughly **two years of work experience (about 2,800 hours)**, and you must commit to returning to Vietnam for two years afterwards. Your chosen course should link to a Chevening priority theme.
- **[GREAT Scholarships (Vietnam)](https://study-uk.britishcouncil.org/scholarships-funding/great-scholarships/vietnam) — partial.** Worth a minimum of **£10,000** towards tuition for a one-year postgraduate course, jointly funded by the UK Government's GREAT campaign and the British Council with participating universities. A small number are offered to Vietnam each year, across selected institutions and subjects.
- **University merit scholarships — partial.** Many UK universities offer international or department awards for Data Science / AI master's applicants (often a £2,000–£10,000+ tuition reduction). These are applied for through the university, frequently with the same deadline as your course application.

## Glowbal's approach
We think of funding in layers: aim for a **fully funded** award (Chevening) if your profile fits, **stack a partial award** (GREAT or a university scholarship) as the realistic baseline, and **budget the remaining gap** yourself. Most successful applicants do not rely on a single scholarship — they combine a partial award with savings or sponsorship.

## Step-by-step guidance
1. **Check Chevening eligibility honestly.** If you have a 2:1-equivalent degree and ~two years of work experience, it is the highest-value option — but it is competitive and deadline-driven. Applications typically open in **August** and close in **early November**, so prepare essays months ahead.
2. **Shortlist GREAT-participating universities** that run a Data Science master's, and note each one's separate deadline.
3. **Apply for university scholarships as you apply for the course** — many are automatic or share the course deadline, so a late application can cost you the award.
4. **Strengthen the profile reviewers reward:** a clear data-related goal, relevant work or projects, and (for Chevening) a credible plan to use the degree back in Vietnam.
5. **Model the funding gap** for each offer — full award vs. £10,000 partial vs. self-funded — before you accept.

## Best options by student type
- **Has 2+ years' work experience:** prioritise Chevening; it is the only routinely fully funded route here.
- **Strong academics, little work experience:** target GREAT and university merit scholarships, which do not require years of work history.
- **Self-funding but cost-sensitive:** stack a university award with a lower-fee programme to cut the gap.
- **Undecided on universities:** let Glowbal match you to Data Science master's that offer scholarships you actually qualify for.

## How to choose
Rank opportunities by **expected value and fit**, not just headline amount: a fully funded Chevening place is worth pursuing if you are eligible, but a £10,000 GREAT award plus a university scholarship can be the more realistic combination for a recent graduate. Always confirm current values, eligibility, and deadlines on the official pages, as cycles and participating universities change each year.

## FAQs
### Can Vietnamese students apply for Commonwealth Scholarships?
No. Vietnam is not a Commonwealth member country, so Commonwealth Scholarships and Fellowships do not apply. Focus on Chevening, GREAT, and university awards.

### Is Chevening realistic without work experience?
It is difficult — Chevening requires roughly two years of work experience. If you are a recent graduate, GREAT Scholarships and university merit awards are the more realistic starting points.

### Do these scholarships cover Data Science specifically?
Chevening and most university awards are open across subjects, including Data Science and AI. GREAT availability varies by university and subject each year, so check the current participating-course list.
