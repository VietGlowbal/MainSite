# UK Computer Science Compared: Manchester vs Birmingham vs Leeds vs Nottingham
## Short answer
For Vietnamese undergraduate applicants, four UK Computer Science programmes show up again and again on sensible shortlists: **Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, and Nottingham**. They are not interchangeable. Manchester is the most selective and most expensive; Nottingham publishes a confirmed, mid-range fee; Birmingham asks for slightly lower grades; and Leeds offers a strong industrial route. The right pick depends on your grades, your budget, and whether a placement year matters to you.

## Who this guide is for
This is for Vietnamese students (and parents) comparing UK Computer Science options for undergraduate entry and applying through [UCAS](https://www.ucas.com/). It focuses on the practical differences that actually change a decision.

## Key differences
- **Entry bar:** Manchester is the hardest (A\*A\*A with A\* in Maths). Birmingham (A\*AA) and Leeds (AAA) are more attainable, which matters if your predicted grades are strong-but-not-perfect.
- **Cost and transparency:** Manchester (£37,800) and Nottingham (£33,000) have **confirmed** 2026/27 international fees. Birmingham and Leeds had **not published** their 2026 international fee at the time of writing — confirm before you commit.
- **Course structure:** Nottingham and Leeds both offer a year-in-industry / industrial route, which can strengthen employability; check each programme's module list for how early it specialises in AI.
- **English requirement:** Manchester's IELTS 7.0 is a real hurdle; the others sit at 6.0, which is easier to evidence.

## Best fit by student type
- **Highest achiever:** Manchester, with Oxbridge/Imperial/UCL as reach options.
- **Value-focused:** Nottingham, for its confirmed, transparent fee.
- **Strong-but-not-top grades:** Birmingham or Leeds, where the typical offer is lower.
- **Employability-first:** Leeds or Nottingham, for the placement/industrial route.

## How to decide
Pick the two or three factors that matter most to you — usually entry bar, cost, and placement options — and rank the four against just those. Then verify the current international fee, the exact offer, and the English requirement on each official course page. Build a UCAS list that spans a reach, a couple of targets, and a safer choice rather than four lookalikes.

## FAQs
### Are these the only UK Computer Science options worth considering?
No — they are four reliable shortlisting choices. If your grades allow, add a reach option such as Imperial, UCL, or Oxbridge; if you want a safer choice, broaden the list further.

### Why are two of the fees missing?
Universities publish international fees on their own schedules. Birmingham and Leeds had not confirmed their 2026 international Computer Science fee when this was written, so we have flagged it rather than guessing.
